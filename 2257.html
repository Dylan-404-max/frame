18 U.S.C. § 2257 Compliance Statement

In compliance with United States law, 18 U.S.C. § 2257, all models, actors, actresses, and other persons who appear in any visual depiction of actual or simulated sexually explicit conduct appearing on this website were eighteen years of age or older at the time of the creation of such depictions.

1. Record-Keeping Requirements

All visual depictions displayed on this site are exempt from the provision of 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. 75 because they do not consist of depictions of conduct as specifically listed in 18 U.S.C. § 2256 (2) (A) through (D), but are merely depictions of non-sexually explicit nudity, or are depictions of simulated sexual conduct, or are otherwise exempt because the visual depictions were created prior to July 3, 1995.

2. Content Uploaded by Users

This website contains user-uploaded content. We do not produce, create, or manufacture any content displayed on this platform. All content is uploaded by third-party users. We are a hosting service provider and do not have control over the content posted by users.

3. Age Verification

We require all users who upload content to affirm that:

  • All persons depicted in the content were 18 years of age or older at the time the content was produced.
  • They maintain records required by 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. 75 for all content they upload.
  • They will produce such records upon valid request by law enforcement or pursuant to legal process.

4. Reporting Violations

If you believe any content on this platform violates 18 U.S.C. § 2257 or depicts persons under the age of 18, please contact us immediately. We will promptly investigate and remove any content found to be in violation.

5. Custodian of Records

The custodian of records for any content produced by this platform is the original producer of the content. For user-uploaded content, the uploading user is responsible for maintaining all required records. We do not maintain records for third-party content.

6. Contact

For any inquiries related to 18 U.S.C. § 2257 compliance, please contact us through the appropriate channels provided on this platform.
